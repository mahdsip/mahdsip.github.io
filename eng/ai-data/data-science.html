Data Science

Maths foundations, Data Science life cycle, Tabular Data practice and course notes

This page is the main entry point for the Data Science material in the site. It now groups four complementary tracks: the Maths base behind machine learning, a Data Science life cycle path built around tabular workflows, practical work around Tabular Data, and the Johns Hopkins content that I already had published.

That structure reflects how I actually approach data work: first understand the mathematical ideas, then organize the end-to-end workflow on data, then solve real problems on structured datasets, and finally deepen the practice with course notes, exercises and reproducible material.

Maths

A structured path through the mathematical foundations that appear again and again in machine learning for tabular problems: vectors, matrices, transformations, probability, statistics, likelihood, loss functions, Bayes and information theory.

  • Maths foundations for machine learning

Data Science life cycle

This block summarizes a practical sequence for tabular-data projects: data preparation, enrichment, feature engineering, missing values, encoding, evaluation, tuning and model selection. The notes come from three PDFs in the NPL bundle and remain linked in their original format.

  • Data Science life cycle

Tabular Data

Most business and healthcare analytics problems start with structured tables, not with flashy demos. This subsection focuses on data preparation, missing values, categorical variables, feature engineering, evaluation and the practical tooling needed to make tabular models reliable.

Johns Hopkins content

After about a year of spare-time work, I completed the Johns Hopkins Data Science specialization in October 2018. Below you can find my notes, assignments, small applications and related references. The specialization includes nine courses plus the final capstone project.
