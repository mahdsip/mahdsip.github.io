Applied Machine Learning & AI

Useful models only appear when data, context and evaluation are handled properly

I see machine learning as an advanced layer in a wider chain: first you need to capture the data correctly, clean it, understand its meaning and build a trustworthy analytical base. Only then does it make sense to train models and expect them to create real value.

My interest in this area comes from both academic training and professional work with medical data exploitation. That is why this page focuses on the parts that actually matter in delivery: data quality, interpretation, reproducibility and usefulness for decision making.

Knowledge base

  • Supervised models for classification and regression, grounded in statistical inference and validation.
  • Unsupervised models to discover structure in data, especially clustering and profiling tasks.
  • Attention to target definition, bias, interpretability and whether a model is actually actionable.
  • Use of historical data to learn patterns and convert information into reusable knowledge.

Algorithms and approaches I have worked with or studied

  • Decision trees and random forests for classification problems.
  • Support Vector Machines and boosting as supervised prediction techniques.
  • Linear, binomial and Poisson models as part of the statistical foundation.
  • Clustering and profiling for segmentation and behavior detection.
  • Neural networks at a conceptual level within the broader set of supervised methods.

What I consider essential before modeling

  • A clean and documented dataset with traceability to the original source.
  • Exploratory analysis to detect outliers, relationships and quality issues.
  • Evaluation criteria tied to the real problem, not just to a single metric.
  • The ability to explain why a model is useful inside the business or clinical workflow.

For me, the best applied AI is not the one that sounds the most sophisticated. It is the one that solves a real problem with understandable data, honest evaluation and a reasonable path into the workflow.
