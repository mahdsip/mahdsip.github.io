An end-to-end view

From capture and cleaning to analysis, modeling and production use

This section brings together the part of my work that is most focused on turning data into decisions. It covers operational and analytical reporting, data science, machine learning, and the software architecture needed to make those capabilities reliable.

Most of the material comes from my professional work on medical data exploitation, from my Master's degree in Information and Knowledge Management, and from the Johns Hopkins Data Science specialization. The goal is to provide a clear map of both learning and practical delivery.

  • Acquisition and integration: databases, ETL, data cleaning, heterogeneous sources and clinical data.
  • Descriptive analytics: operational reporting, dashboards, scorecards, multidimensional analysis and data warehousing.
  • Advanced analytics: statistical inference, regression, clustering, profiling and supervised models.
  • Value delivery: reproducible notebooks, visualization, sample apps and APIs or services to share results.
