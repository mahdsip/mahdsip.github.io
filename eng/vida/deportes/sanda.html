Shaolin Sanda

Ten years training Chinese martial arts in Madrid

For 10 years I practised Sanda, a Kung Fu-based contact sport, at Shaolin Temple Spain in Madrid under Sifu Carlos.

What is Sanda?

Sanda (also known as Sanshou) is a Chinese combat system that combines striking techniques (punches and kicks) with takedowns and throws. It is the combat modality of modern Wushu and is practised with protective gear on a ring or mat.

My experience at Shaolin Temple Spain

For a decade I trained regularly at Shaolin Temple Spain, a traditional Chinese martial arts school led by masters trained directly at the Shaolin Temple in China. Sifu Carlos was my main teacher and taught me not just fighting techniques but also the philosophy and discipline behind the martial arts.

Spanish Wushu Championship 2017

In June 2017 I had the chance to compete in the Spanish Wushu Championship in the Sanda category. It was an intense and rewarding experience that put everything I had learned over the years to the test.

What Sanda taught me

  • Discipline: Regular and demanding training
  • Respect: Towards teachers, peers and opponents
  • Control: Physical and mental control under pressure
  • Perseverance: Constantly pushing personal limits
  • Humility: There is always more to learn

The combination of football and Sanda over so many years gave me a solid foundation in physical fitness, mental discipline and competitive spirit that I bring to every aspect of my life.
