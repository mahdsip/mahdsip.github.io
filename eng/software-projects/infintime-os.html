InfiniTime OS on PineWatch

Open-source operating system for a smartwatch

What is InfiniTime

InfiniTime is an open-source operating system for the PineTime smartwatch by Pine64.

PineTime: open hardware

The PineTime is a low-cost smartwatch with up to 7 days of battery life and Bluetooth 5.0.

My experience

I run InfiniTime on my PineTime paired with my Xperia 10 running Sailfish OS through Amazfish.

Integration with Sailfish OS

  • Call and SMS notifications
  • Music control
