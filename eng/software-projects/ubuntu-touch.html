Ubuntu Touch

Ubuntu Touch on Google Nexus and tablet

Google Nexus with Ubuntu Touch

I experimented with Ubuntu Touch on my Google Nexus. It's a mobile operating system based on Ubuntu, originally developed by Canonical and now maintained by the UBports community.

The experience was interesting, if limited. Ubuntu Touch offers a unique gesture-based interface and the promise of convergence between mobile and desktop.

Installation on a tablet

I also installed Ubuntu Touch on a tablet, where the experience was more satisfying thanks to the larger screen taking better advantage of the interface.

Highlights:

  • Edge-swipe gesture-based interface
  • Scopes for organising content
  • Full Linux terminal
  • Native apps and web apps

Limitations I ran into

  • Limited app ecosystem
  • Some hardware compatibility issues
  • Battery less optimised than Android

Despite the limitations, Ubuntu Touch is a fascinating project that shows it's possible to have a truly free, Linux-based mobile operating system.
